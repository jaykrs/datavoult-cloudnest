# Payments and invoices

## Ways to pay

### Online payment (Razorpay)
Pay by card, net banking, wallet or UPI through Razorpay. The payment is verified automatically and your order is recorded straight away; our team then confirms it and provisions your server. Card details are handled by Razorpay and are never stored by DataVoult.

### UPI or bank transfer
1. At checkout choose **UPI** or **Bank transfer**. You see the payee details and a unique **payment reference**.
2. Pay the **exact amount shown** and include the reference in the payment remarks.
3. Submit the **transaction reference** (UTR) in the form on the order page.
4. Our team checks it against the bank statement and **approves** it, usually within one business day. You get an email when it is approved or if we could not verify it.

Payments that do not match the amount are rejected with a reason; contact us with your transaction reference and we will sort it out.

## After payment

- **Online payments** are verified automatically and your order is recorded straight away. Our team then confirms (approves) the payment before setting up your server.
- **UPI / bank transfer** create your order when we approve the payment.
- Your service is then set up by our team; you are emailed the access details when it is ready (see [Your service and access details](/docs/your-service)).

## Invoices

An invoice is generated automatically once a payment is confirmed:

- **With GST** — shows the taxable value, the GST rate and amount, and the total.
- **Without GST** — for products with no GST.

Find all your invoices under **Invoices** in your dashboard. You can add your billing name, GSTIN and address under **Settings → Billing** before you pay so that they appear on the invoice.

## Cancellation

You can cancel a service from **Services**. Cancelling stops renewal; the service keeps running until the end of the period you paid for.
