# DataVoult documentation

DataVoult sells cloud infrastructure you can order online and manage yourself: **VPS servers**, **Docker hosting** and **business email**. This guide explains, in plain language, how each part of the platform works.

## What you can do

- **Order a service** — pick a product and plan, choose how many days you want (minimum 30) and pay online, by UPI or by bank transfer. See [Products, plans and pricing](/docs/products-and-pricing).
- **Get your server details** — once your payment is confirmed and your service is set up, you receive the IP address and login by email and see them in your dashboard. See [Your service and access details](/docs/your-service).
- **Deploy your own application** — build and run a Next.js or Node.js repository on your VPS, or a Docker image from your GitHub repository on Docker hosting, with HTTPS. See [Deploying applications](/docs/deploying-applications).
- **Download invoices** — a GST or non-GST invoice is generated automatically after every payment. See [Payments and invoices](/docs/payments-and-invoices).

## How an order flows

1. You choose a product and plan and the number of days.
2. You pay (Razorpay online payment, UPI or bank transfer).
3. For UPI and bank transfer, our team checks the payment and approves it. Online payments are confirmed automatically.
4. Your order is recorded and our team sets up your server.
5. You receive your access details by email and in your dashboard under **Services**.
6. Your invoice becomes available under **Invoices**.

## Need help?

Use the [contact form](/contact) or the **Support** page in your dashboard. Please include your order reference.
