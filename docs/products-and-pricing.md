# Products, plans and pricing

## Products

| Product | What it is | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VPS** | A virtual server with root/SSH access | Websites, APIs, databases, anything that needs a full server |
| **Docker hosting** | A managed Docker host for containers | Running containerised apps built from your own Dockerfile |
| **Business email** | Mailboxes on your own domain | Professional email for your team |

Every product has one or more **plans** that differ in resources (for example CPU, memory and storage).

## Pricing

- Prices are shown **per day, in Indian rupees (₹)**.
- You choose the number of days when you order. The **minimum is 30 days** and the maximum is 3 years.
- **Total = price per day × number of days**, plus GST where it applies.
- You can renew at any time before or after expiry to extend your service.

### GST

Each product is configured as either *GST inclusive* (the listed price already contains GST), *GST extra* (GST is added at checkout) or *no GST*. The checkout shows the exact breakdown before you pay, and your invoice repeats it.

## Availability

Some plans and products have limited capacity. When a plan is **sold out** it is hidden from the catalogue and cannot be ordered; other plans of the same product stay available. If every plan of a product is sold out, the product is shown as unavailable. Existing customers can always renew the plan they are on.

## One subscription per product

You can hold one active subscription per product at a time. To change plans, renew or contact support.
